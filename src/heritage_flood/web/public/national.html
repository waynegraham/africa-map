Evidence and its limits

832 source records are retained; 831 were screened against the checked country representation. One coastal record remains excluded and unscreened. The JRC/CEMS GloFAS v2.1 model uses a native grid of approximately 90 m. The 10/100/500-year return periods represent modeled hazard, not observed April 2022 flooding. Water/artifact flags qualify samples; absent depth does not establish safety. National boundaries: geoBoundaries / OCHA ROSEA / MDB, source CC BY 3.0 IGO. NASA landslide values and detailed local road/bridge geometry are excluded from this public package.

Locations, institutional function and source tags remain independently unverified. Point/grid context does not establish building damage, collection value or preparedness. Missing observations remain unknown.
