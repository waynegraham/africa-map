Evidence and limits

Institution inventory

Public OSM snapshot; incomplete and uneven coverage. Coordinates remain independently unverified. Way/relation locations are source bounding-box centers, not reviewed building footprints. Suspected duplicates remain separate.

Country and provinces

geoBoundaries gbOpen; underlying OCHA ROSEA / South African Municipal Demarcation Board sources. Year represented: 2020. Technical checks passed; current legal boundaries are unverified. This representation excludes the offshore Prince Edward Islands. Excluded source records are retained for review.

Modeled river hazard

JRC/CEMS GloFAS v2.1, native 3 arc-second grid (approximately 90 m). Point samples and a geodesic 100 m context are separate. Permanent-water references and suspected model artifacts qualify the results. Missing depth is unknown, not safety. A 100-year model corresponds approximately to 1% annual exceedance probability under its assumptions.

This static river model does not establish observed flooding, damage, local stormwater or coastal hazard, institutional vulnerability, or future climate-change exposure. No climate-baseline interval has been independently verified here.

Regional observations

The linked eThekwini work reviews April 2022 imagery. Other regions need their own event selection, track/coverage checks and independent dated evidence. No nationwide event labels or machine-learning predictions are assigned.
