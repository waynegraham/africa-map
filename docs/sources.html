Sources, methods
and the limits of inference

A map is an argument about its evidence. These notes make the sources, gaps and boundaries of that argument available for inspection.

The atlas supports exploratory research and further data collection. It does not establish institution damage, safety or investment priority. Full technical methods and source registrations accompany the code in the research repository.

Reading the evidence

Each explorer offers Map and Globe. Projection changes the display, not source coordinates, model samples or scientific measurements. Globe provides broad geographic orientation and becomes locally flat at high zoom. The flat camera is restored when returning to Map.

The maps keep these forms of evidence separate. They answer different questions and should not be read as interchangeable measures of risk.

Source records
Where an institution is mapped. A source point is not a verified building footprint, and an inventory is not a census.
Observations
What a sensor or other record supports at a particular time. Missing observations cannot establish that no flooding occurred.
Modeled hazard
What a specified model represents for a return period. A grid sample is not a measured depth at a building.
Future scenarios
Conditional regional comparisons within a product and model. These do not establish a forecast or property-level outcome.

No composite risk score, climate-change attribution or investment ranking is provided. Building condition, collection value and preparedness remain unknown unless supported by independent information.

Institution inventory

Institution locations and tags are © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Original OSM IDs and source attributes are retained in the map data. Ways and relations may use bounding-box centers. Names, institutional function and coordinates have not been independently verified; suspected duplicates remain separate records.

The Africa snapshot contains 4,655 POIs. The national snapshot retains 832 source records, of which 831 were screened against the checked country representation. One coastal record remains excluded and unscreened. The eThekwini pilot retains 28 selected records.

None is a complete institutional census. Counts describe the retained source records.

The landing-page plate plots all 4,655 retained Africa source points in the Equal Earth projection (EPSG:8857). Blue distinguishes South Africa by its source-country code; it does not encode exposure. No boundary or flood layer is used in the plate. Its source checksum and display parameters are retained.

Models and surroundings

Native resolutions and source dates constrain interpretation. Resampling for display does not add scientific detail.

  • River flood hazard

    European Commission JRC / CEMS GloFAS river flood hazard v2.1: static modeled depths in metres, native 3 arc-seconds (approximately 90 m), displayed for 10-, 100- and 500-year return periods. These are not observed April 2022 water depths. Permanent-water and suspected-artifact flags qualify samples.

  • National and provincial boundaries

    geoBoundaries / OCHA ROSEA / South African Municipal Demarcation Board: national and provincial 2020 boundary representation, source metadata reports CC BY 3.0 IGO. Display geometry is simplified; mainland representation excludes offshore Prince Edward Islands. Current legal boundaries are unverified.

  • Land cover, surface water and residential population

    ESA WorldCover Consortium 2021 v200 (10 m; CC BY 4.0), EC JRC / Google Global Surface Water v1.4 (30 m; 1984–2021), and EC JRC GHSL P2023A (100 m; residential population 2020) describe surroundings. Population is not visitor count or exposed population. Acknowledgement and provider terms apply.

  • Roads and bridges

    Road and bridge summaries use © OpenStreetMap contributors via Geofabrik, ODbL 1.0. Mapped ways do not establish routing, closures, emergency access or completeness.

Event and climate observations

The April 2022 event is examined in eThekwini. Those observations are not transferred to the rest of South Africa.

  • Rainfall estimates

    CHIRPS v2 final, Climate Hazards Center: public-domain gridded rainfall, approximately 5.6 km. The pilot compares 11–13 April 2022 with the same dates in 1991–2020. Regional estimates can miss local extremes and are not gauge observations.

  • Historical climate and nearby burning

    Climate histories use Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF ERA5-Land (approximately 11 km; processed for this project), CHIRPS, and NASA LP DAAC MODIS MCD64A1 v6.1 burned area (500 m; 2 km context). Outdoor heat and humidity do not establish indoor conditions or collection damage. Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for use of Copernicus information in this project.

  • Sentinel observations and comparison evidence

    Sentinel-derived QA and acquisition identities refer to EU/ESA/Copernicus Sentinel observations accessed through Earth Engine. No imagery figure or SAR overlay is redistributed here. The satellite analysis is exploratory and has not established confirmed flood or building-damage labels.

    UNOSAT product 3283 is linked as comparison evidence; its copyrighted imagery is not copied and exact institution coverage remains unresolved. Satellite interpretation is not field ground truth.

Future scenarios

Scenarios remain separate from historical observations and static baseline hazard. Compare within the same model and product.

  • Regional flood scenarios

    WRI Aqueduct Floods V2: approximately 1 km regional scenario grids, 100-year return period. Provincial summaries use one illustrative river GCM and coastal median sea level without subsidence. They do not describe full model uncertainty or institution exposure.

  • Future regional heat

    Regional future heat comparisons use NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 / Climate Analytics Group, native 0.25° grid, SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5, three illustrative models. Exact model names, years and paired changes appear in the explorer. The model range is not a probability interval or the full ensemble.

What the public site includes

These are filtered presentation snapshots of fuller local research bundles. Public access does not by itself grant redistribution rights. Material with unresolved reuse conditions is withheld rather than presented as cleared for publication.

  • Municipal layers and mixed-source figures

    The municipal boundary and historical floodplain have no established redistribution license. Their geometries, overlap and distance results, mixed-source comparison figures, SAR overlays, municipal-mask rasters and municipal rainfall aggregates are excluded from the public pilot. Identity-document excerpts and proposed identities are also withheld.

    This does not imply no flooding. Per-institution public-source model and rainfall samples remain separate context indicators.

  • Landslide values and detailed local downloads

    NASA static landslide susceptibility is omitted from the national snapshot because the explicit file license has not been independently verified. Road/bridge detail geometry and analytical download files are not included in this presentation package. Full local research bundles remain unchanged.

Provenance and reuse

Snapshot manifests retain source bundle hashes, input and code provenance, omissions and public file checksums. Snapshot build times are retained rather than represented as new observations.
