Satellite observations

Optical observations on 13, 18 and 23 April have no conservatively clear samples in the two inspected contexts. The SAR comparison uses 12/24 March and 5 April against 17 April 2022.

The original two-site SAR experiment has not established a distinctive flood signal. The remaining 26 point contexts have generated comparisons and per-site quality checks; individual interpretations are pending. Some early optical contexts retain partial clear samples. Later observations cannot rule out transient flooding during the earlier peak.

Regional context

JRC river depth is static modeled hazard at approximately 90 m. CHIRPS rainfall is a regional estimate at approximately 5.6 km. Missing or flagged model samples do not establish safety. The Context view keeps these sources separate from satellite changes.

Institution locations

What further research can resolve
