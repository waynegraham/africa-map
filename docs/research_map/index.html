Evidence and its limits

Municipal boundary/floodplain geometry, overlap/distance results, mixed-source figures, SAR overlays, municipal-mask rasters and aggregates, and proposed identities are withheld. Source points, reviewed OSM outlines and permitted per-record context remain. The satellite experiments have not established confirmed flood or damage labels. Sentinel QA refers to EU/ESA/Copernicus observations via Earth Engine; imagery is not redistributed. UNOSAT institution coverage remains unresolved.

Locations, institutional function and source tags remain independently unverified. Point/grid context does not establish building damage, collection value or preparedness. Missing observations remain unknown.
