A note on interpretation

An invitation to investigate, with the limits in view.

These maps bring together evidence with different purposes, dates and resolutions. A point near a modeled flood cell does not establish building damage. Outdoor climate indicators do not describe collection storage conditions. Missing observations remain unknown.

The intended outcome is better questions and further data collection: verified institution locations, independent dated flood evidence, and information about buildings, collections and preparedness.
