# Limitations

- The OSM inventory is incomplete and uneven; absence of a mapped institution does not establish absence of heritage.
- Some institution points are bounding-box centers. Coordinates do not establish building footprints or collection locations.
- Municipal floodplain mapping may have incomplete coverage, mixed capture dates, or undocumented assumptions. These require inspection.
- Short-lived flooding can occur between satellite acquisitions. Cloud, vegetation, urban geometry, and terrain can limit detection.
- No detected flooding is not evidence of no flooding when observations are missing or unsuitable.
- Regional hazard grids do not establish building-level inundation depth. Resampling cannot create finer evidence.
- Public sources may not describe institutional vulnerability, collection significance, past damage, or preparedness.
- Satellite-derived assessments may share imagery with the analysis and are not necessarily independent validation.
- Historical observations do not by themselves attribute floods to climate change or predict future hazard.
- Publicly accessible services may restrict redistribution. Record permissions before publishing derived or source artifacts.
