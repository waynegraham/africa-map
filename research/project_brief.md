# eThekwini heritage flood-exposure proof of concept

## Purpose

Assess whether publicly accessible datasets can support credible flood-exposure screening for cultural institutions. Help heritage professionals and prospective research funders identify where additional investigation is warranted.

## Scope

Start with museums, libraries, and archives in the existing South African OSM inventory, selected using the eThekwini municipal boundary. Establish location quality and municipal-floodplain exposure before attempting an April 2022 satellite reconstruction. Add future climate scenarios and evaluated ML only after a checked baseline exists.

## First milestone: feasibility audit

1. Obtain a municipal boundary with verified provenance, CRS, and reuse conditions.
2. Select and inspect institution records, preserving original identifiers and suspected duplicates.
3. Assess municipal floodplain coverage, date, assumptions, CRS, and access terms.
4. Inventory Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 acquisitions around April 2022. Define an appropriate pre-event baseline only after checking coverage and seasonality.
5. Identify comparison evidence and document its independence and limitations.
6. Record a decision on event suitability before producing exposure claims.

## Deliverables and success criteria

- A checked institution inventory with stable IDs and quality flags.
- A dataset register distinguishing candidates from verified inputs.
- Reproducible exposure measurements linked to sources and processing runs.
- A map separating evidence layers and making missing observations visible.
- A short case study identifying research questions and additional data needs.

Success means a reviewer can trace a displayed measurement to its inputs and method, understand uncertainty, and identify what further data collection would resolve. A trained ML model is not required for the first milestone.

## Exclusions from the initial milestone

Building-damage estimates, inferred collection values, composite risk scores, investment rankings, climate attribution, automated AI narratives, and continent-wide processing.
